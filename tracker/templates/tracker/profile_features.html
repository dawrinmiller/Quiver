How the score works

Your score is out of 100. It looks at four parts of your job search:

  • Applying regularly — up to 40 points: How many applications you have sent recently and how often you apply.
  • Getting interviews — up to 25 points: How often your applications lead to interviews.
  • Getting offers — up to 15 points: How often your interviews lead to job offers.
  • Keeping track — up to 20 points: Recording notes, events, or feedback, or moving forward to interviews and offers.

Applications marked Preparing do not count yet. Waiting for an employer to reply does not count as a failure. If you have only a few applications, the score has less information to work with.

Use the score and tips to help plan your next steps. The score cannot tell you how skilled you are or whether you will get a job.
